Two corrections were found by the claim-by-claim verification of the eight claims this investigation had previously reported as unverified. Both are naming or version errors, and both are reflected in the published text:

  • BIAS is CVE-2020-10135, not CVE-2020-12351. CVE-2020-12351 is a BlueZ flaw — improper input validation that enables kernel privilege escalation, sometimes called BleedingTooth — not an impersonation attack. The error appeared in one register entry, not in the articles.
  • BLASTPASS affected iOS 16.6 and earlier. iOS 16.6.1 is the fix, not an affected version: the victim ran 16.6, and Apple shipped the patch in 16.6.1.

Reports that a specific CVE, date, figure or attribution is wrong are welcome and are corrected with a dated note. The full machine-readable log accompanies the publication package.
