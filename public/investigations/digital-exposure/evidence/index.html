Evidence and method

ARTIore Research Desk · 2026-10-26

Every factual claim in this investigation carries an evidence row. The row states the verdict the corpus’s own record reached, the evidence label that follows from it, the source, and the note that tells a reader what the claim does and does not support. The rows are not a summary: they are the audit trail, placed directly underneath the claim they describe.

Where the verdicts come from

The corpus holds three per-claim verification records, and they do not overlap. The event ledger covers 125 claims, the Part 3 batch ledgers cover 68 others, and the corpus’s own queue report covers a further 13. A claim’s verdict is copied from whichever record covers it; no verdict is recomputed, and two records are never averaged into one.

What the labels do not mean

A — Confirmed means an authoritative source supports the claim as written. It does not mean the claim has been tested by the authors. B — Demonstrated in research means the mechanism has been shown in published research and the claim as originally worded was too broad. C — Plausible but unverified means the mechanism is credible but the specific claim is not established by the cited source — the note says what would establish it. U — Unsupported means the claim is contradicted or has no support, and it is not presented as fact anywhere in this investigation. O — Outdated or superseded means a newer version exists and is used instead.

Where the sources come from

Source identifiers are namespaced and never merged: r15: for the R15 package register, src: for the publication register, legacy: for the retired numbering, and url: for a URL in the citation pool. An identifier marked locator only has been identified precisely but its deep link was not fetched. The source register lists every source in full, with its date and status.

Corrections

Errors are corrected with a dated note, not silently. The corrections log is public: Corrections.
