Evidence labels

VERIFIED — named, limited check performed on the stated source. SUPPORTED INFERENCE — reasoning supported by evidence but not directly established. DISPUTED — material credible disagreement. UNVERIFIED — no sufficient check. BELIEF / INTERPRETATION — philosophical or religious framing, not empirical proof. CORRECTED — earlier statement amended with a retained record.

Every factual claim should have a source, date checked, scope, limitation and correction path. Multiple repeated claims do not equal independent verification.
