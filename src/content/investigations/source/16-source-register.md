# Source Register

Every material factual claim in the investigation maps to an entry below. Entries record the publisher, publication date, type, what the source supports, and a link or locator.

## Link policy

- **Verified link:** the URL was checked at publication.
- **Locator:** the source is identified precisely — publisher, title, date, document type — but a stable deep link could not be independently verified at publication. The locator gives the archive or search path rather than a guessed URL. We do not fabricate deep links.
- CVE identifiers resolve at `https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/<CVE-ID>` and `https://www.cve.org/CVERecord?id=<CVE-ID>`.

**Type key:** **P** = primary (official record, vendor advisory, court filing, regulator, first-party statement); **S** = secondary (journalism, analysis, industry report); **R** = research (peer-reviewed or independent technical research).

## Limitations of this register

1. **Secondary sources are marked.** Where a figure comes from journalism or an industry report rather than an official record, the entry says so.
2. **Estimates vary.** Cryptocurrency totals differ between Chainalysis and TRM because of clustering methodology and valuation timing; both are presented.
3. **Breach counts change.** Figures such as Marriott's 383 million or Change Healthcare's 190 million are the latest published at the time of writing and may be revised.
4. **Locators are honest, not lazy.** A locator means the source exists and is precisely identified, but a stable deep link could not be verified. We prefer a locator to an invented URL.
5. **Absence is not evidence.** A topic with few sources (for example, hotel-network incidents in Africa) reflects reporting gaps, not necessarily lower risk.
