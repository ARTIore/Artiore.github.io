## C046 — NIST SP 800-207

> “Zero trust (ZT) is the term for an evolving set of cybersecurity paradigms that move defenses from static, network-based perimeters to focus on users, assets, and resources.”

> “Zero trust assumes there is no implicit trust granted to assets or user accounts based solely on their physical or network location (i.e., local area networks versus the internet) or based on asset ownership.”

Source: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/207/final (August 2020).
